    Gully Doga day in the life of a street dog in India

    You are Bhura, the brown Indie dog everyone in the mohalla knows. Autos honk, cows sit in the middle of the road, the chai wallah has biscuits and the halwai has samosas he really should not leave on the counter.

    Keep your belly full and your water up, nap in the shade when the sun gets fierce, make friends (dog and human), and stay out from under the buses.

    Sit in front of someone kind and they may pet you or share a snack. Grumpy people shoo. The other street dogs guard their patches — greet them calmly, keep them company, share food, and they become friends who follow you around. On a full belly, E picks food up instead of eating it, so you can carry it to someone. Aunty in Pump Wala Chowk feeds the dogs at 7:30 am and 6:30 pm.
